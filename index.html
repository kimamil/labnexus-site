The problem

The science is rarely the bottleneck

In most organisations we are called into, the constraint is not the chemistry or the biology. It is the systems around it, the handovers between teams, and the number of people who have to agree before anything can move.

  • Decisions that need two teams have no forum designed to carry them
  • Knowledge sits in silos and leaks at every handover
  • "We'll fix it later" is not available in a validated environment

The answer

From diagnosis to running change

The failure mode of consulting is the handover: a partner sells the thinking, a team you have not met delivers it, and the insight leaks out somewhere in between. We are built so it cannot.

Science
A peer to your scientists — trained as one.
Technology
Fluent enough to challenge vendors and architects — without being one.
People
Leaves a mechanism your teams run, not a dependency on us.

How it works

Four stages, one owner

  1. 01

    Listen

    Before proposing anything: an interview pass across the organisation, to separate a missing mechanism from a problem that only looks like one.

  2. 02

    Order the real issues

    A facilitated working session that puts the issues in the order they can actually be solved — and a written diagnosis the sponsors can act on.

  3. 03

    Deliver alongside

    Hands-on, inside the programme: decision cadence, ways of working, and the delivery itself — not a slide deck handed over the wall.

  4. 04

    Hand over a mechanism

    A standing way of working your own people run, each part with a named owner and an explicit criterion for handing it over.

FAQ

Asked before you book

Who will we actually work with?

With the person who diagnosed your problem, from the first interview to the running change. We take on only as much as we can carry that way.

Which sectors do you work in?

Pharma, life sciences and regulated industry, where GxP, validation and change control are the environment we work in, not a surprise that resets the plan. And Swiss SMEs on information security, where management needs a governance it can steer rather than another tool.

Which languages?

German and English. We are based in the Basel region.

Do you implement software?

No, deliberately. We decide with you what to build or buy, steer the vendors who build it, and stay accountable until it runs. We sell no development hours, so our advice never depends on them.

How does an engagement start?

With a 30-minute call. If we are not the right answer, you hear that in the same call.
