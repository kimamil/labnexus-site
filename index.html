Profile

I'm Manuel Kammermann.

Founder · LabNexus GmbH · Basel

I translate between the lab, the shop floor, management and their suppliers.

Digital projects in the life sciences rarely stall on technology. They stall between the people who have to agree on them: research needs one thing, production another, management signs off a third — and suppliers and regulators read the result their own way.

That gap is where I work. I talk to researchers as a molecular biologist, to management like a peer, and to suppliers as someone who has been one. I turn what each side needs into one plan that is compliant and secure — and I stay until it runs.

Recent mandates

What I was brought in to do

Context

  • A structured content authoring solution is available
  • Source data is available at unknown quality, granularity and format
  • End-to-end structured data pipelines are established as proofs of concept for selected, simple scopes

Sound familiar?

  • Initial proofs of concept showed promising results — the programme has stagnated ever since
  • Efforts to expand the data pipelines beyond the first manufacturing site grow linearly instead of scaling
  • Each more complex submission chapter adds complexity to the authoring solution
  • Agentic workflows lack the process, modality and filing-strategy understanding to produce the expected narratives

How I help

  • Establish what the source data can carry before automating it — quality, granularity and format, chapter by chapter
  • Sequence chapters by data readiness, not ambition: scale where the pipeline holds, stop where it does not
  • Bring the authors' process, modality and filing-strategy knowledge into the design of the agentic workflows
  • Run it as one product across teams, so the programme decides on value rather than on demos

Context

  • A manufacturing execution system rolls out across a multi-site production network
  • Vendor, build streams and validation each run their own delivery cadence
  • A multi-year roadmap has to be steered and reported to senior management

Sound familiar?

  • The technology plan is sound — the interfaces between streams are where the time goes
  • Decisions that need two parties to agree have no forum, so they surface late and get re-opened
  • Leadership asks for a transparency the plan cannot give

How I help

  • Listen first: a structured interview pass from the shop floor to leadership
  • A facilitated working session that puts the real issues in the order they can be solved
  • A common way of working with named owners and explicit handover criteria — built to run without me

Context

  • A new workflow solution replaces established ways of working
  • Project delivery, change management and testing usually sit with different roles

Sound familiar?

  • The system goes live; the way people work does not change
  • Nobody is accountable for the distance between delivery and adoption
  • Training and testing start when the project is nearly over

How I help

  • One accountable role across project management, business analysis and change
  • Stakeholders, training, and QA and test management planned from day one, not after the build
  • The decision about the system and the decision about how people will work with it, made by the same person in the same week
